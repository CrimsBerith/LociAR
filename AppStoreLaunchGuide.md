YAKLAŞTIĞINDA HABERİN OLSUN

Because you are testing from Apple Park in Cupertino, California, we have designed the app to be 100% testable anywhere in the world—including indoors at an office desk—without needing to be physically located in any specific city.

2. HOW TO TEST INDOOR / DESK AR PINNING (At Apple HQ / Office Desk):
1. Sign in with the test credentials above.
2. Grant Camera and Location (When In Use) permissions when prompted.
3. If the Google AR notice appears, tap "Tamam" / OK.
4. Tap the "+" (Paylaş / Share) tab at the bottom.
5. Point your iPhone camera at your desk, a table, or the floor.
6. Move the device slowly until the reticle turns green ("Gerçek yüzey hazır" / Plane detected).
7. Tap "Yüzeye sabitle" (Pin to surface) and scan the surface for 2 seconds.
8. Tap "İçerik ekle", write a test note (e.g. "Apple Review Desk Test"), and tap "Yüzeyde yayınla".

3. Tap the "Keşfet" (Discover) tab to view global public posts and test the search bar.

- Account Deletion: Go to Profile -> Settings -> "Hesabı sil" to test immediate and permanent account deletion.
