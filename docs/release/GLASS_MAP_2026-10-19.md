## Behaviour

- Smooth irregular glass lenses: brand mint → aqua → warm amber on a fixed count scale. No perpetual pulse; Reduce Motion/Transparency and increased contrast are supported.
- Heat uses coordinate-weighted density cells. Legacy coordinate-less members backfill through daily reconciliation; until then their cells retain the coarse fallback centre.
- Close zoom shows real 25 m groups, including **10 notes at one coordinate**. Screen-space collision grouping combines separate nearby places until zooming separates them; a real co-located group stays together.
- Tapping a real group opens its sheet, with most likes first, newest/id tie-breaks and 50-row pagination. A 120-note fixture returned every note without duplication.
- Other people's location-note bodies are redacted without a fresh on-site fix, regardless of group size. Viewport coordinates and previous unlocks cannot grant a later remote read. Authors retain their own content access.
- Default access is within 15 m of the group's circle, plus at most 5 m accuracy tolerance. Fix age is at most 10 s and accuracy at most 35 m. Small groups get a server-computed circle before persistent spot membership forms.
- Direct Firestore note reads remain owner-only. On-site reads grant a location-free, TTL-protected 30-minute comment session; this grant never unlocks note bodies remotely. Author/account deletion removes relevant grants.
- The group sheet hides loaded rows after leaving, stale fixes or permission revocation. Anonymous mutes and profile blocks remain independent. AR's existing dense-spot policy is retained.
