# Brand campaigns and selected-user notifications

## Current flow

1. Create a managed public identity in **Users**, then register the brand in **Brand campaigns**. Managed identities have disabled Auth and no administrator role.
2. Optionally grant an existing verified account editor membership. `submitBrandCampaign` lets that account submit a text-only draft through the future brand client. It cannot approve, select recipients, read personal data or send notifications. No separate brand portal is shipped yet.
3. Create a draft with message, discount code/terms, retail coordinates, radius, start/end and performance thresholds. Approve or pause it in the panel. Protected zones are blocked; the emergency switch stops activation and sends. There are no photos, videos, social links or new drawings.
4. Scan nearby eligible recipients. Each batch checks 25 location reports. Reports must be fresh within two minutes, unexpired, accurate within 200 m, and match the user's current location-sharing consent revision. Distance plus reported accuracy must fit the campaign radius. Suspended, deleted and managed content accounts are excluded.
5. Choose a recipient explicitly and use **Assign offer and notify this user**. The server rechecks consent, location and performance. This commits one private assignment, an in-app activity, an audited notification job and its daily quota together. A retried request reuses its operation key.
6. A Functions worker rechecks permissions, consent, active campaign/brand, location and performance before FCM. The user can tap the notification/activity to view their sponsored offer, terms, expiry and walking directions. The offer also appears in their nearby map/AR results. Administrator placements stay explicitly approximate.

**Users → Private data → Send this user a notification** also queues a custom text message for one opted-in user. The administrator writes in the recipient's language. Campaign pushes use generic, server-localized text in the app's 12 languages; the lock screen carries no coupon, performance score or coordinates.

## Consent, scope and controls

- **Notifications** is off by default and covers social alerts, location notes and administrator messages. Brand campaigns and offers also need the separate, off-by-default **Campaigns and offers** switch with its own consent text (App Store Guideline 4.5.4); the server stores marketing consent as `notifications && promotions`, and arrival and departure consent follow it. The combined notice covers voluntarily shared location and approved-post performance for offer selection. Administrator location sharing remains separate. iOS notification permission is independent; in-app activities do not require a registered push token.
- Brand editors do not get Firebase Admin credentials, admin roles, personal locations or audience lists. `brands.write`, `campaigns.write`, `campaigns.audience`, `users.location.read` and `notifications.send` are separate panel permissions. Initially only super admins can select recipients and send. The existing ad-operations role has campaign read access only.
- Campaigns and target assignments are server-only, separate from public `posts`. The callable returns a post-compatible sponsored projection only to an assigned, opted-in recipient while the campaign and brand are active. No recipient identifier is included in the projection. Ordinary social interactions on these private offers are not enabled.
- Eligibility counts approved public posts among the latest 100 by creation time. The like threshold is the best single post's count minus admin display offsets; legacy manually edited metrics without offsets do not qualify.
- Queue admission allows at most three promotional/administrator jobs per user per UTC day. Each job/device has at most one FCM attempt, claimed before sending. A process failure at that boundary can lose a push; the in-app activity persists. `accepted` means FCM acceptance, never proven device delivery or reading.
- Target assignments expire at campaign end. Expired data is denied before TTL cleanup. Jobs expire after one day; attempt and view receipts after 30 days; quotas after two days. Account deletion removes the user's assignments, views, memberships, jobs and receipts. Disabling Notifications or Campaigns and offers cancels pending sends, campaign monitoring and future offer access, and deletes the learned usual place on the phone.

## Zone campaigns (journeys)

A campaign with steps (`audience: 'zone'`) reaches everyone with Notifications and Campaigns and offers on; nobody is picked by hand. Each step (up to five) has a trigger, optional zone, earlier-step requirement and validity, days/hours in the campaign time zone, channels (notification, map note, AR note), title, message, code (none, shared, unique per person), frequency (once, daily, every N hours) and an optional total limit.

- **Leaves usual place.** iOS visit records teach the phone where it usually spends the night (≥ 3 nights in 21 days, device only, never uploaded or labelled home). The phone monitors a 150 m region there; on exit it checks locally that the campaign area covers that place and sends only its current fix. The server checks the fix lies within the campaign area + 1 km.
- **Enter / stay / leave a zone.** Zones are circles (15–2,000 m) or streets (2–20 points + width). Entry uses the location-note rule (uncertainty may touch the zone, ≤ 20 m tolerance, fixes ≤ 35 m); stay needs continuous presence for the set time; leave needs having been inside and then clearly outside.
- **Admission.** `recordCampaignStep` checks consent (`promotions` + `arrivals`, or `departures` for leave steps), kill switch, live campaign and brand, schedule, geometry and confirmation, step order and validity, frequency and budget, then issues the code and records progress in one transaction. Map/AR channels expose the private offer card with the person's codes.
- **Keeping phones current.** `readCampaignZones` returns nearby steps from a coarse fix. Activating a campaign sends a silent push to geohash-4 area topics; phones also refresh after ~500 m moves, when leaving a 1–3 km boundary region and from a periodic background task.
- **Panel.** Campaigns → *Zone campaign steps* edits steps on an OpenStreetMap picker (click to set a circle centre or add street points in order; the campaign area is dashed). **Send test to my phone** queues the step's title and message (a unique code shows as `PREFIX-TEST`) to a chosen LociAR account through the normal opt-in and three-per-day checks, without zone checks, codes or counters. The funnel shows sent and redeemed per step; *Redeem a code* marks counter codes as used.

## Location delivery modes

Each assignment selects `map`, `immediate` (default), `arrival` or `departure`. Map mode exposes the private offer in the recipient's nearby map/AR results without a notification. Immediate mode queues FCM. Arrival mode assigns the offer without a push; the iPhone presents a local notification after server admission. The audience radius (100–5,000 m) selects nearby users. The OS wake-up radius (100–500 m, default 150 m) only starts a short precise GPS check. Actual entry uses the location-note rule: `distance ≤ entry radius + min(GPS accuracy, 20 m)`, accuracy at most 35 m, entry radius 15–100 m (default 30 m). Passing the shop on foot or by car counts; the old 2–5 m diameter almost never fired with real phone GPS. Map and arrival audiences may use a recipient's last voluntarily shared location up to the campaign's `audienceMaxAgeMinutes` (default 24 h), so people who live or work nearby can be assigned a reminder for when they pass later; push-now and departure assignments still need a location from the last 2 minutes. No place is labelled home.

Arrival uses the same master Notifications switch; legacy `arrivals` fields remain for compatibility. Background operation requires iOS Always location permission; foreground monitoring can work with When In Use. A best-accuracy local GPS window lasts at most 120 seconds per nearby wake, with the iOS location indicator. Its measurements do not update administrator location data. `readCampaignRegions` returns only assigned, live, unblocked offers. The native service selects up to 20 nearest regions, refreshes while open, removes revoked/expired assignments on sync and stops monitoring on local opt-out or sign-out. New offers must synchronize while the app is open before background entry can trigger them.

`recordCampaignArrival` validates the account, live campaign/brand, current consent, assignment mode, assigning super-admin authority, emergency switch, an entry fix at most ten seconds old, a confirmation started at least two seconds earlier and location uncertainty entirely inside the actual entry circle (exit fixes may be up to 60 seconds old). Coordinates are transient: they are neither retained in arrival records nor added to the administrator location snapshot. A transaction claims at most one arrival per campaign/user across devices, creates in-app activity and reserves the shared three-per-UTC-day promotional quota. It returns the approved custom reminder (or a generic localized reminder when blank) and the private offer deep link. Network/process interruption after admission can lose the alert; activity remains. No offline alert is emitted and GPS is not merchant redemption proof.

GPS cannot establish doorway/indoor entry. No beacon or other merchant hardware is added. GPS is usually 5–30 m off; very large uncertainty (over 35 m) suppresses the reminder. iOS may delay or suppress region delivery, including after force-quit. Physical-iPhone checks must cover background wake-up, reduced/precise location, notification permission, opt-out and deep linking. The background location capability is limited by the native 120-second window; no location permission prompt is added at launch.

## Custom messages and leaving an area

The panel accepts a moderated notification title (80 characters) and message (240 characters) for immediate, arrival or departure offers. URLs are rejected. Write in the recipient's language; custom text is not automatically translated and can appear on the lock screen. The notification and in-app activity open the assigned private offer, whose location remains the brand's branch. Blank messages retain the generic localized reminder.

Departure uses the same Notifications switch; legacy `departures` fields remain for compatibility. It never infers or labels a user's home. Assignment captures the recipient's fresh, voluntarily shared location into a server-only `campaign_departure_regions` record, with a 100–500 m exit radius. The region expires at campaign end or within 24 hours, whichever is earlier. Coordinate admission requires uncertainty fully inside at setup and fully outside at exit. A separate consent epoch prevents an old origin from reviving after opt-out and opt-in. Opt-out invalidates every origin immediately, deletes up to 100 records in the preference transaction, and remaining expired data follows TTL. Account deletion removes all origins.

The iPhone must synchronize while inside the original area and observe its inside state before arming exit monitoring. Cached armed identifiers survive an iOS region wake-up; a first outside state does not trigger a departure. `recordCampaignArrival` retains its name for client compatibility and accepts `transition: entry|exit`. It validates current consent, mode, region expiry, campaign/brand/recipient/assigner availability and shared quota. Departure, like arrival, is admitted at most once per campaign/user. The origin is deleted after admission, switching modes or expiry; exit fixes are not stored in the administrator location snapshot. Newly assigned reminders cannot trigger before the phone has received and armed the region. Store entry and departure remain approximate GPS boundaries, not redemption proof.

## Boundaries and release

The discount code is informational text governed by the displayed terms. Merchant/POS verification, unique one-time coupons, redemption budgets, billing and a brand-facing portal are separate future work. Start/end dates gate access; scheduled campaigns need an administrator to queue notifications after their start, and there is no automatic scheduled send. A brand membership can be revoked from the brand form or all submissions stopped by pausing the brand.

Deploy `submitBrandCampaign`, `onAdminNotificationQueued`, `readCampaignRegions`, `recordCampaignArrival`, updated existing callables, the App Hosting panel, iOS build, and `firestore.indexes.json` with the new indexes/TTL policies. Confirm FCM/APNs, App Check and live TTL readiness separately. Emulator tests use injected FCM senders and never contact live FCM. Physical iPhone/APNs and physical AR behavior remain release checks.
