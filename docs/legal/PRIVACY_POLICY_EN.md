# LociAR Privacy Policy (Draft)

Effective date: set before production launch. Controller, postal address, support email, and jurisdiction must be filled with real values before publication.

## Data we process
LociAR processes account identity (Apple, Google, or email provider identifier), public profile handle, camera and photo content selected to create AR posts, approximate and precise location while the app is in use, device/diagnostic data, in-app engagement, moderation reports, and AR calibration metadata when a trusted operator calibrates a surface.

## Why
We use this data to authenticate users, show nearby surfaces, create and moderate user content, protect sensitive locations, prevent abuse, improve stability, and respond to legal or safety requests.

## Sharing and retention
Google Firebase (Google Cloud) processes backend data and hosts uploaded post assets. We do not sell personal data. Public posts and public profile handles are visible to viewers after moderation approval. Delete requests remove or de-identify account data subject to legal, fraud-prevention, and backup retention requirements.

## Your controls
Viewing is public. Creation requires a verified account. You can report content, block a user, request account deletion in Profile, revoke permissions in iOS Settings, or contact support. Location, camera, and photo permissions are optional; relevant features will not work without them.

## Safety
Posts in protected zones are hard-blocked. 18+ content is not accepted. Every public post enters manual moderation before discovery. Platform links are opened through their official experiences; LociAR does not extract or rehost third-party streams.
