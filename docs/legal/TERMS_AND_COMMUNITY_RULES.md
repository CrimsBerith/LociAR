# LociAR Terms and Community Rules (Draft)

Complete legal entity, contact, governing law, and effective date before launch.

- Do not create, publish, or calibrate content on protected zones, private property without permission, schools, hospitals, places of worship, government sites, museums, or unsafe locations.
- Do not publish sexual/18+ content, harassment, hate, threats, illegal content, impersonation, copyrighted material without rights, or deceptive advertising.
- Do not use LociAR while driving or in a way that creates a safety risk. Follow property rules and local law.
- Public posts are manually reviewed. LociAR may remove content, restrict accounts, preserve evidence for safety, and cooperate with lawful requests.
- Report, block, and contact paths are available in the app. Reported content is triaged by severity; imminent safety or illegal content receives priority.
- Third-party links remain subject to their providers' terms. LociAR does not scrape, extract, or rehost platform video streams.
- AR alignment is best effort. arkit_world_locked means a tracked, on-site calibration in the current native AR workflow; cross-session accuracy may require relocalization or recalibration.
